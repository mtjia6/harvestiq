Dividend reinvestment (DRIP)

An automatic reinvestment is a purchase the wash-sale rule counts. Declare it so we can warn you to turn it off before harvesting.

Wallets & transfers

A transfer moves lots between your wallets carrying cost basis and holding period with them — it is not a sale, nothing is realized. Sells always draw only from the wallet they happen in (per-wallet basis, Rev. Proc. 2024-28).
